# Family Trip to Lilliput and Blefuscu

## Overview

Four days across two island cities, built around a 4-year-old's nap — which
needs a bed, so far-away places get half days — and Gulliver's wheelchair
needs. The family flies into Lilliput from Paris, spends two days there,
then takes the morning ferry to Blefuscu for two more. Each city's days are
built around the places the family named, plus the suggestions they picked.

## Destination Research

### Neighborhoods

Lilliput's Main Square district was chosen for lodging: flat, wide
boulevards (wheelchair-friendly) and walking distance to the Royal Gardens.
Mentioned as stroller/wheelchair-friendly by 2 independent sources (a
Lilliput-with-Kids travel blog and a general accessibility guide). In
Blefuscu, the family named the Old Harbour, and the inn they're considering
is on its edge.

### Must-Visit Highlights

- **Royal Gardens** — mentioned by 3 independent sources (a Tier 1 travel
  guide, a local family blog, and a travel-subreddit thread) as the top
  family activity in Lilliput. Free entry, flat paths throughout.
- **Imperial Fleet Museum** — mentioned by 2 independent sources as
  Blefuscu's best indoor stop for children. Step-free entrance per its own
  site.

### Lilliput: places

- **Named by you:** Royal Gardens (attraction); Mildendo Sea Cliffs
  (attraction, about 45 minutes from the hotel).
- **Picked from suggestions:** Taverna Minor and the Museum of Miniatures
  (both near the Royal Gardens); the Royal Library children's room (near the
  Royal Gardens).
- **Suggested, not picked:** Lilliput Toy Market (near the Royal Gardens).
- **Meals:**
  - Day 1 lunch: Taverna Minor (picked with the Royal Gardens suggestions).
  - Day 1 dinner: The Thimble Café (also offered: Main Square Deli).
  - Day 2 lunch: Pocket Pizza (also offered: Main Square Deli, The Thimble
    Café).
  - Day 2 dinner: Main Square Deli (also offered: Pocket Pizza).

### Blefuscu: places

- **Named by you:** Old Harbour (neighborhood); Imperial Fleet Museum
  (attraction); Emperor's Palace (attraction — see Needs Attention).
- **Picked from suggestions:** Old Harbour Bakery and Little Lighthouse
  Park (both in the Old Harbour); Harbour Carousel (near the Imperial Fleet
  Museum).
- **Suggested, not picked:** Blefuscu Harbour boat tour (in the Old
  Harbour; about 2 hours on the water, plus an hour each way to the
  outer-harbour pier).
- **Meals:**
  - Day 3 lunch: Old Harbour Bakery (picked with the Old Harbour
    suggestions).
  - Day 3 dinner: Harbour Chowder House (also offered: Lighthouse Grill).
  - Day 4 lunch: Lighthouse Grill (also offered: Harbour Chowder House).
  - Day 4 dinner: none — you skipped this one (offered: Harbour Chowder
    House, Lighthouse Grill).

### Flights

Already booked by the traveler (LP101 from Paris), so no flight search was
done. The flight's row on Day 1 shows how a cross-time-zone time is written:
departure in Paris time, arrival in Lilliput time.

### Safety Notes

The Lilliput and Blefuscu government travel advisories (country-level) list
no warnings for either island. They don't assess individual neighborhoods,
so they say nothing specific about the Main Square district, the Old
Harbour, or the Sea Cliffs.
